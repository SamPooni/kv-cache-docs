RoPE Is Not a Universal Locality Guarantee

Position can be one input to a prefetch heuristic, but RoPE itself does not prove that a fixed ±W window captures a fixed fraction of future attention. Any locality window and hit-rate benefit must be learned or measured from real attention traces.
