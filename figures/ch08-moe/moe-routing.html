Expert Residency Uses the Same Placement Logic

For mixture-of-experts models, routing probabilities can be treated as future-use hints for expert weights or state. Expert count, top-k routing and fetch latency are model/platform specific; v5 reports no MoE speedup.

→

No zero-latency claim: pre-resident experts avoid a fetch for that invocation, but residency itself has capacity and movement costs.
