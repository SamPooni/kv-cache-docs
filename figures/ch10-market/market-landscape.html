KV-State Management Landscape

This landscape is intentionally capability-based. Product specifications and research results change quickly; v5 does not use competitor superlatives or vendor-gap claims as technical evidence.

Memory expansion / pooling
Examples include CXL-attached DRAM and pooled-memory platforms. Their existence establishes additional capacity options, not KV-cache performance.
Inference runtimes
Paged KV allocation, prefix caching, batching and scheduling reduce fragmentation/recomputation, but behavior is workload- and version-dependent.
KV disaggregation research
Research explores CPU/DRAM/SSD/CXL/remote-memory placement, prefetch and near-memory processing. Results are not directly comparable without matching workloads and hardware.
Application-aware caching
Emerging agent workloads expose reuse signals such as session identity, prefixes and tool-return timing. These can inform policy but require independent validation.
Positioning rule: v5 claims a measured GB10 observation, a corrected simulator and a KV object/control-plane design. It does not claim to be the first, only or universally superior KV-cache solution.
